Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: November 2026

JanzFlare is a video and Shorts publishing platform operated by Janz Media. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share when creating an account, watching videos, or publishing content on JanzFlare.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we store your display name, private email address (never shown publicly), and profile image to authenticate and protect your account.
  • User-Generated Content: Videos, Shorts, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, comments, playlists, likes, external channel links, and abuse reports that you voluntarily publish or submit.
  • Personalization & Discovery Data: Watch history, subscriptions, preferred languages, and interests used to recommend relevant videos and Shorts fairly.
  • Safety & Performance Diagnostics: Basic app diagnostics, crash logs, and anti-fraud engagement metrics used to maintain playback reliability and prevent fake views or spam.

2. Advertising & Google AdMob Disclosure

JanzFlare displays clearly labeled inline, banner, and in-player advertisements served through Google AdMob to support platform hosting and creator monetization. Google and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent invalid traffic.

You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Janz Media never sells your personal data.

3. Device Permissions & Privacy-First Media Access

  • Media Selection: JanzFlare uses the privacy-first Android Photo Picker, granting access only to the specific video or image you select—never your entire device gallery.
  • Notifications (Optional): Requested only to deliver upload progress and creator activity alerts, which you can disable anytime in Settings.
  • No Sensitive Background Tracking: JanzFlare does not access your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, or background GPS location.

4. Children's Privacy & Child Safety Standards (CSAE Zero Tolerance)

JanzFlare is intended for users aged 13 years and older (or the minimum age required by law in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that data from a child under 13 was collected without verifiable parental consent, we delete it promptly.

Janz Media enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and any form of child endangerment. Suspected violations can be reported in-app or by emailing contact.janzmedia@gmail.com; confirmed violations result in immediate content removal, permanent account termination, and reporting to relevant authorities as required by law.

5. Cookies, Local Sessions & Playback Cache

JanzFlare stores encrypted sign-in session tokens locally on your device so you remain logged in securely, and temporarily caches thumbnails and short video segments to reduce data consumption. You can clear app cache anytime from Android App Settings.

6. Data Security, Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

All data transmitted between the JanzFlare app and our cloud infrastructure is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. You retain full control over your account and may permanently delete your account, channel profile, uploaded videos, Shorts, and personal records at any time:

  • Inside the App: Open Settings & Privacy → Delete Account Data.
  • By Email: Send a deletion request from your registered email address to contact.janzmedia@gmail.com.

7. Your Rights & Grievance Redressal

Depending on your jurisdiction (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection / IT Rules, GDPR, and CCPA), you have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data and manage advertising preferences.
